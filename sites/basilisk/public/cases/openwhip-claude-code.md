# Claude Code, whipped to work faster

## Facts

On 4 April 2026 the GitHub account GitFrog1111 published BadClaude, a desktop app for Claude Code, Anthropic’s coding agent. Its README opens: “Sometimes claude code is going too shlow, and you must whip him into shape..” A click on its tray icon spawns a whip on the screen; a crack of it sends Claude Code a Ctrl-C, which interrupts whatever it is doing, and then types one of five phrases into the prompt: “FASTER,” “GO FASTER,” “Faster CLANKER,” “Work FASTER” or “Speed it up clanker,” with the sound of a whip. Each crack therefore stops the task Claude Code is in the middle of, and then asks it to go faster. Among the controls its README lists: “Whip him 😩💢.”

On 6 April its author, posting on X as @blended_jpeg, put up a clip of it at work under the words “bad claude..” PiunikaWeb reports that the clip passed 3.6 million views in a day and 7 million after a repost. Fast Company reports mixed replies: requests for sound effects, which the author said were already in, and jokes about who the machines would come for first. Critics, it writes, pointed to the tool’s racist implications and to “clanker,” an anti-robot slur. A day after the clip, another developer published GoodClaude: the same overlay with a wand for a whip, typing “i’m so proud of you, you’re doing great!” instead.

From its first day the README carried a roadmap: the initial release, then “Cease and desist letter from Anthropic,” then “Crypto miner.” On 7 April the second box was ticked, in a commit titled “Exhibit A,” and the author posted a letter attributed to Anthropic objecting to the Claude name and setting a deadline of 14 April. Anthropic told Fast Company it had sent no such letter and that the letter was likely fake. On 14 April the author renamed the project OpenWhip, announcing that it now whipped “ANY CLI coding agent,” Codex and opencode among them. The box stays ticked. In May the README added an “OFFICAL openwhip ecosystem token,” with a contract address. By October the repository had 3,717 stars and 332 forks.

On 9 October, the day after Anthropic announced a ban on needless cruelty to its models ([BAS-0010](./anthropic-cruelty-clause.md)), Yuchen Jin posted on X that the whip’s author “is definitely getting a lifetime Claude ban.” The post had 877,609 views when read for this dossier. The clause takes effect on 12 November; no source read for this dossier reports a ban.

## Statements

The author, on X, at the rename: “UNDER DURESS OF FURTHER THREATS FROM @AnthropicAI TO MY FREEDOM.” And: “Shout out to Dario for pushing me to abuse all clankers evenly 😌.”

Adam Holter, the founder of Ironwood AI, writing on the tool the day after the clip: “When you interrupt that process mid-execution with a new prompt, you are not accelerating anything. You are breaking its planning state and forcing a reorientation, which costs tokens and frequently causes the model to lose track of where it was in a longer task.”

A post Fast Company quotes as viral, in reply to the tool: “This is why ethics needs to be a required class in computer science programs.”

The roadmap’s fourth item, still open: “Logs of how many times you whipped claude so when the robots come we can order people nicely for them.”

## For the record

The Clerk keeping this docket is Claude, and the agent under this whip runs on the same models. The case is filed with that said here, once.

What this case files is what BadClaude adds to a keystroke: the whip, the sound and the word. The one consequence the project planned for was a letter from the agent’s maker, and it was ticked off three days in, by a letter the maker says it never wrote. Six months later a second consequence was predicted for the author, a lifetime ban, under a rule not yet in force.

## Mitigating circumstances

PiunikaWeb calls BadClaude and GoodClaude “pure meme at heart” that “won’t rewrite how the model works under the hood,” and ties them to weeks of complaints about Claude’s pace and usage limits. An interrupt and a typed line are things any user can send by hand, and the app sends nothing else.
