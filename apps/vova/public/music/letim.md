<!-- lang:en -->

This is the first song written on Suno 3. As I remember it, we were on a “visa
run” in Trebinje, I tried a couple of generations, and after Suno 2 my mind was
simply BLOWN. And then the hills and mountains of Herzegovina filled the picture
in and came out as the lyrics. That was well before the “release” date —
somewhere around February 2024 — but then, as with
[Crossroads](./crossroads.md), I kept rewriting the words and redoing the ending.

допеределывал концовку.
