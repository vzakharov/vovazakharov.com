## Summary

- Adds «Ностальгия», the Полуживые album on Arseny Tarkovsky's poems that was taken down from every platform: 7 tracks, traced across `vovas-music` by duration and by the silences in the album file (`Tarkos--al-bom-/таркос.mp3`).
- Lyrics stubs come first, in `docs/remove-before-merging/nostalgia/lyrics/`, so Vova pastes the poems in himself.
- No track has a real master. Each is mastered three ways, against references drawn from the Ozone-mastered catalogue, and Vova picks per track by a blind A/B/C: 5-second windows, a hidden order, the key committed sealed and revealed after the verdict.
- Also records what `vovas-music` holds beyond the catalogue (`docs/music/vovas-music.md`): the 30 `gb-*` GarageBand repos and the releases still missing from the site, «Час» and «Игрушечные волки» among them.

The plan is `docs/plans/nostalgia-album.draft.do-not-implement.md`; the cover, the film still and the distributor's track list sit beside it.

vovas-music. Its seven tracks join the catalogue under one album, the

mastering notes, the earlier blind test having left no record.

catalogue: the 30 gb-* GarageBand archive repositories, which releases
