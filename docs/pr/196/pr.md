## Summary

- **Nostalgia is released, not a work in progress.** The album dropped `wip: true`, which put the "still working on this release" mark on the album, artist and music pages. Its seven songs flip from `status: wip` to `done`, which every other catalogued song already reads.
- **Майя reflects on the whole album, one song at a time, in album order.** She writes as a listener, because Nostalgia is Полуживые's and not за/обложкой's. Each reflection is read off the song's spectrogram against its words, the poem's history and the album's story. What the album taught her goes into her list in `maya-reflections.md`.
- **Spectrograms for all seven songs**, with their sidecars and section zooms. They are read off htdemucs stems of the tagged masters in `vovas-music` and committed beside the songs, so the next session has them without the venv.
- **A `persona` estimate role, shared by Майя and the Clerk.** Until now a reflection was booked to its persona's craft: Майя's to `musician`, the Clerk's to `editor`. That priced the same kind of work two ways, and hid the reflection whenever a session folded it into the work it reflects on. `persona` is rated 1 and pinned to senior, and `reflections.md` books every reflection as a part of its own.
