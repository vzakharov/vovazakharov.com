## Decided

- On the opening clump the two doors stand one above the other on screen
  (~5 px apart across), so any course that sweeps across must come back:
  it is a loop out to one side and back in. There the runner **turns once**,
  at the loop's far side, where the course really turns back; it never
  flips on the near-vertical stretches. A course between doors apart on
  screen never flips.
- The sweep is bought with a longer least span (2.4 s), not a faster pace:
  call 8's `RUN_PACE` 0.6 stays, and call 25's "no shorter than about a
  second" is a floor.
- W4's overlap was two runs from the **back** door 0.4 s apart (the back
  tap ran its own mouse, then the front tap — front empty — called the
  back's second), drawn together because the old course was a short dip at
  the front door's foot. Not one door starting two runs at once.
