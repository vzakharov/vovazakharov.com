# R7 — the run crosses the screen: hand-over

Finishes R6's next step (`r6.md` § "Left").

## Done

- **Step 1**: `bowedPath` bows **aside** first, until the aside-bowed course
  alone is `least` long, then toward the eye from that point until its
  middle is `clearance` nearer than the nearer front (still never past half
  the way to the eye). The least length now comes from the aside bow, so it
  always shows as sweep across the screen; the toward bow only adds to it.
  The clearance is measured from the aside-bowed middle, so it holds on
  either side of the chord. `RUN_BOW` 1.8 → 1.
- **Facing** (`facingAlong`, in `mouse-run-course.ts`, replacing
  `run-front.ts`' `facingOn`): the way the course last ran clearly across
  the screen (`acrossOn` ≥ `TURN_ACROSS` 0.25 of its unit way), else the way
  it first will, else the way it runs most across anywhere. This is
  hysteresis read purely off the path: no flip on a course that crosses the
  screen, none where the course runs toward or away from the eye.
- `play-runs.ts` notes each runner's x sweep (`drawn from x … to … px`).

## Decided

- On the opening clump the two doors stand one above the other on screen
  (~5 px apart across), so any course that sweeps across must come back:
  it is a loop out to one side and back in. There the runner **turns once**,
  at the loop's far side, where the course really turns back; it never
  flips on the near-vertical stretches. A course between doors apart on
  screen never flips.
