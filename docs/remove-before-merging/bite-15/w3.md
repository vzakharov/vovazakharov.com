## Decided

- The probe reads `house.worm.trip.source` / `.target` and gives them out
  as the spec's `from` / `to`.
- `HouseWorm.painted` (head middle and girth in the house's graphics px,
  set in `shown`) is what the probe reads the worm's head off — the path is
  in the cap's frame, which the browser side cannot map without
  `capFrame`. The mouse's `drawnHead` is the precedent.
- `capLeft` is measured by `topAt` over a 24×24 grid on the cap hit area's
  box: of the points finding the mushroom or one of its windows, the share
  finding the mushroom. Points under an insect or off the mushroom do not
  count either way.
