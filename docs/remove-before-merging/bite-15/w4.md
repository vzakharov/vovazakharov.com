# W4 — looking at the worm, and tuning it

## Done

- Step 1: the meadow play ran green on tabL and phoneP (R6's door fix
  already in). Seen in W3's frames and a fresh run: stretched out (the out
  frame) the five segments stood apart with gaps toward the tail — beads on
  a string — and the wriggle swung them further apart. At 5 px it read, but
  thin against a ~30 px window.
- Step 2 (357c94e): the wriggle is a travelling wave. `wriggle(elapsed,
index)` gives each segment the swing of the one before, `WRIGGLE_LAG`
  (π/4) behind, `WRIGGLE_DEPTH` 0.5 → 0.2 girth (was alternating ± per
  segment), `WRIGGLE_RATE` 10 → 8. `WORM_SEGMENTS` 5 → 8, `TAIL_TAPER`
  0.65 → 0.7, so each segment overlaps the next stretched out and at every
  moment of the wriggle; `worm.test.ts` asserts it (gap < 0.95 × the two
  radii, stretched and drawn in, every 2 ms). `to-check.md`'s wriggle line
  asks whether the wave is visible.
- Step 3: with eight segments each over its own ink the body read as a
  dark-ringed millipede, so `paintWorm` lays every segment's ink first and
  every fill over it: one inked pink body, the band a collar behind the
  head. Constants: `WORM_GIRTH` 0.3 → 0.35 × `PANE`, `WORM_GIRTH_LEAST`
  5 → 6 px, `WORM_LENGTH` 4 → 4.5 girths, `WORM_PACE` 0.35 → 0.25,
  `WORM_CRAWL` [1.2, 3] → [1.4, 3.5], `INCH_PERIOD` 0.45 → 0.4,
  `INCH_SQUEEZE` 0.35 → 0.4 (exported; the tests read it and `WORM_PACE`
  instead of literals). Why the pace: at 0.35 an inch's step was about
  three squeezes, so the "holding" end slid at a third of the leading
  end's speed — a glide, not an inch. Now across a three-window dome
  (~0.43 units) the step (~0.068) is about the squeeze (0.063): the holding
  end all but stands still. The trip is ~2.3 s (was ~1.8). Inks unchanged.
- Step 4: frames (W3's six `git rm`'d):
  - `w4-tabL-out.png` — the worm half out of the square window, one smooth
    pink body with an eye, no gaps.
  - `w4-tabL-crawl.png` — at 0.8 s, drawn in mid-inch on the cap's face
    by the white spot, head and eye leading left, the collar behind it.
  - `w4-tabL-wriggle.png` — 0.13 s into a wriggle: the outline ripples,
    the body stays whole.
  - `w4-phoneP-crawl.png` — the same on the phone, 6 px, plain on the red.
  - `w4-phoneP-peek.png` — the back cap's lone window: the worm straight
    up out of it, looking about.
