# R6 — the bowed course, the flee frame, looking and tuning: hand-over

## Done

- **Step 1 (call 25)**: `model/mouse-run-course.ts` (new): `RunPath`, a
  quadratic curve `from` → `to` drawn toward `bend`; `bowedPath(from, to,
eye, clearance, least)` puts the curve's middle `clearance` nearer the eye
  than the nearer front (measured radially from the eye, as `doorFront` is),
  then bows farther toward the eye until the course is `least` long, never
  past half the way from the nearer front to the eye; `pathLength`;
  `alongPath(path, progress)` → point and unit tangent, by arc length (32
  pieces). `RUN_BOW` 1.8 door widths (a runner's length, R5's measure).
  `mouse-run-clock.ts`: `RUN_LEAST` 1 s, exported; `pathBetween(from, to,
eye)` = `bowedPath(…, RUN_BOW · wider door, RUN_PACE · RUN_LEAST)`;
  `runnerAt` takes the path, its point and heading from `alongPath`.
  `mouse-runs.ts`: `runLength` is the course's length at the start; each
  frame re-bows the course from the ends stood afresh and the eye now;
  the doorway look faces the course's tangent at its start.

  door the mouse comes out of. `--plays meadow --screens tabL` green.

## Decided

- Nearer is measured against the door **fronts**, which already stand a
  stem's half width nearer than the feet, so the call's "nearer than the
  nearer foot" holds with that much to spare.
- The bow is re-made each frame from the current eye, as the ends are; the
  run's length (its duration) is fixed at its start.
