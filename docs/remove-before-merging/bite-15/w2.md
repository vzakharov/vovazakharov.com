## For W3's probe

All on `shown.house` (TS-private fields read as the probe reads `hit`):

- `topAt`: the house graphics now takes window taps, so `top ===
shown.house.graphics` means door _or_ window. Tell them apart with
  `house.takes(local)` (private; local = the graphics' inverse of the
  point), which returns `'door'` or the window's index.
- `windows(id)`: `house.reaches` (circles in the graphics' frame, r in its px;
  multiply by `graphics.scaleX` for CSS px) and `house.face` (the outline
  they are cut to).
- `worm(id)`: `house.worm.trip` (`tappedAt`, `from`, `to`, `girth` in units:
  times `size × scaleX` for px), `house.worm.trips`,
  `house.worm.at(scene.clock)` → phase and head/tail arc lengths;
  `house.worm.shown(...)` is the paint path (it records state; the probe
  should not call it).

## Decided

- A door tap that `tappedPart` gives the door but the bed's `nearestDoor`
  gives another house's door goes to nobody in this house (as before).
- `trips` counts only trips with a target, not peeks.
- The eye sits forward and to the upper side of the head; on a peek (going
  straight up) it is centred, shifted by `look`.
- Inks: `worm` 0xf4b0a8, `wormBand` 0xd8_80_84. Tests: saturation at most half
  the fly agaric's and chanterelle's, ink contrast ≥ 4.5 (band ≥ 3), the band
  deeper and within 15° of hue.
- No `reachCircle` in `tap-reach.ts`: the window's test is a callback, so a
  true circle needs no widened polygon.
