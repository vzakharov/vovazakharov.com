# R4 — tap on a runner, the patter, the clock split: hand-over

## Done

- **Step 1 (call 23)**: the run's clock (`RUN_PACE`, `RUN_LEGS`, `RunLeg`,
  `RunOpening`, `RunCourse`, `runDuration`, `runAt`, `RunMoment`, `RunEnd`,
  `endOf`, `widthAlong`, `Runner`, `runnerAt`) moved verbatim from
  `model/mouse-run.ts` to `model/mouse-run-clock.ts`, its tests to
  `mouse-run-clock.test.ts`; `mouse-runs.ts` imports from both.
  `RUN_PACE` went with the clock, its only reader. 240 + 229 lines.

- **Step 2 (call 12, the tap)**: `hop(since)` in `mouse-run-clock.ts`
  (0.3 s, half a runner's width high, a sine arc) + tests. Each run's
  graphics is interactive on `run.hit`, a circle `tapReach(width / 2)` round
  the body's middle (`RUNNER_MIDDLE` 0.3 widths over the feet, raised with
  the hop), in the graphics' own frame, re-set every `move`. A tap sets
  `run.tappedAt` and squeaks; `move` adds `hop(t − tappedAt)` to `raised`.
  The run's clock never reads it. Depth decides who takes a tap (Phaser's
  top-only), and the meadow's tap returns on anything under the finger, so
  the ground's picker never sees it. A hidden runner takes no tap.
  `MouseRun` gains `Pick<Tapped, 'tappedAt'> & WithCircleHit`.

- **Step 3 (call 12, the patter)**: `MeadowSound.patter(pan, level)` after
  `squeak` (+13 lines): one soft triangle tick, 30 ms, pitch drawn
  1.5–2 kHz falling to 0.6 of it, peak `0.05 · level`; dropped before
  `start`, as `step` is. `mouse-runs.ts`'s `move` ticks it every
  `PATTER_EVERY` (0.08 s) of the run's clock while the run is in its `run`
  leg and its runner drawn, `run.pattered` holding the last tick; pan
  `panOf(eye, point)` (the insects' pan, which is the runner's screen
  side), level `min(1, CLUMP_DISTANCE / place.distance)`.

## Decisions

- The patter ticks by time (0.08 s), not per footfall: the legs scissor by
  distance over a stride of 0.55 runner widths, which at `RUN_PACE` on a
  small door's mouse is tens of footfalls a second — a buzz, not a patter.
- Pan by `panOf` (spec-runs call 14) rather than a fresh screen-x formula.
