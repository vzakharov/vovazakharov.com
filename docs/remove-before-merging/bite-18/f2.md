# f2 — a stale tab must not overwrite a newer meadow

Review comment 4178121299. Done in one step; nothing left.

- `open-kept.ts`: the keeper writes through a store wrapper that remembers
  the record that last landed (the opened raw record to begin with);
  `Opening.overwritten()` reads the stored record back and compares it, by
  `JSON.stringify`, with that memory.
- `meadow-keeping.ts`: from a hide (after its keep) keeping is paused; on
  show, `overwritten()` decides — reload when true, else unpause and pay any
  keep asked meanwhile. Hidden again before the answer: stays paused. A
  failed check is reported and keeping stays paused for the load.
- Picked read-back over a `BroadcastChannel`: a suspended iPad tab may never
  receive (or may handle after its first poll) a message posted while it
  slept; the read-back runs on the show itself, and the pause from the hide
  means no write can slip in before it.
