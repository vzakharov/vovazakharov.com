[^serotonin-en]: A coinage on the model of _listopad_, “leaf-fall,” and _zvezdopad_, “star-fall.”

[^rhyme-en]: _Nenast’ye_, “foul weather,” rhymes with _schast’ya_, “happiness”: the one cloud in the song is there only to rhyme with it.

[^kittens-en]: Nearly a quote from Zinaida Alexandrova’s children’s poem “Kittens,” where it is a _sunny_ bunny — a patch of reflected sunlight — that leaps off the wallpaper to chase you.

[^village-en]: A nod to the punk band Sektor Gaza: “It’s good in the village in summer, / Shit sticks to your shoes.”

[^spoon-en]: A line from Zinaida Alexandrova’s children’s poem “The Big Spoon.”

[^pooh-en]: A nod to the song about the empty pot in the Soviet Winnie-the-Pooh cartoon: “An end to your sufferings and disappointments, / And good weather sets in at once.”

[^sho-en]: The echo plays on _sho_, the colloquial “what” the chorus rhymes on.
