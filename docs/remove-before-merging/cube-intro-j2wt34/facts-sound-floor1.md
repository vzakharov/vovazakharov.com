## Part 1 — Sound and voice

### How sound is made

- **Every sound is synthesised in code, as a `Float32Array` of samples, on the main thread, then played as an `AudioBuffer`.** No audio asset, no `AudioWorklet`, no `OfflineAudioContext`, no `OscillatorNode`. Web Audio is only the mixer: `AudioBufferSourceNode` → `StereoPannerNode` → `GainNode` → master `GainNode` (0.8) → `DynamicsCompressorNode` → destination, plus one `ConvolverNode` per place for reverb (`features/sound/scape/ui/soundscape.ts`).
- The DSP kit is `features/sound/dsp/lib/dsp.ts` (barrel `features/sound/dsp/index.ts`): `noiseOf(seed)` (mulberry32 white noise in [-1, 1)), `chance(noise)`, `filterOf('low'|'band'|'high', rate)` (state-variable filter, called per sample as `f(sample, hz, q)`), `dying(t, dies)`, `swell(x)`, `ringing(struck, t, tune)`, `samplesIn`, and `sampled(seconds, rate, (t, i) => number)` which runs `tanh` on each sample and fades the last 4 ms.
- Three sub-slices under `features/sound`: `cues` (one-shots), `scape` (the rig and the room tone), `dsp`. They enter each other by barrels (`../../dsp`, `../../cues`). The feature's public API is two names: `createSoundscape`, `bearingOf`, and the type `SoundCue` (`features/sound/index.ts`).
- Determinism: a synth takes `(…params, seed: number, rate: number)` and draws only from `noiseOf(seed)`. At play time the seed is `word(`${cue.kind}/${count}`)` for the body's own cues (`word = pathHash('0')`, a running counter per soundscape) and `word(key)` from the planted seed for a room's sound (`hearing.ts`), so two viewers hear the same knock.
- Nothing sounds before a user gesture: `hear(canvas)` calls `scape.unlock()` on the canvas's first `pointerdown` or any `keydown`; `cue` and `play` return silently unless `context.state === 'running'`. **A cue fired before the first gesture is dropped, not queued** — an intro that opens on surf needs the gesture first (or the bed starts late).

### (b) A new continuous bed (surf, wind, a hum at a point)

**What exists is one bed, the "place", and it is not positioned.**

- `Sound = { footing, hum, air, echo }` (`shared/lib/acoustics.ts`) is a floor's whole acoustic description; each theme carries one as `theme.sound`. Floor 1 (Reception): `{ footing: 'tile', hum: 100, air: 0.15, echo: 1.6 }`.
- `scape.setPlace(sound, scale)` is called **every frame** by `hearing.body` with `themeOf(pose.n).sound`. Same `hum`/`air`/`echo` as last time → free. Different → `settle()`: the old `Place` is ramped to 0 over `CROSSFADE = 1` s and its loops stopped; a new `Place` is built (`placeOf`) and ramped to 1. That is the only cross-fade.
- A `Place` = a level `GainNode`, a `ConvolverNode` whose stereo impulse is `impulse(sound.echo, seed, rate)` from `scape/model/tone.ts` (noise dying over `echo` seconds, unit energy, min 0.08 s), and looping buffers: `air(seed, rate)` (4 s of low/band-filtered noise, tail folded over head for a seamless loop, cached per context) at `AIR_LEVEL 0.16 * sound.air`, and `hum(hz, seed, rate)` (2 s, whole number of periods, harmonics + ballast buzz at 2.8 kHz) at `HUM_LEVEL 0.05` when `sound.hum > 0`. Each loop goes dry to the level and at `TONE_REVERB 0.5` into the reverb. Loops are started by `loopInto(context, buffer, level, outs)` — a fixed gain, **no panner, no handle kept to change it**.
- **Listener position and orientation are not fed to Web Audio at all.** No `AudioListener`, no `PannerNode`. Spatialisation is computed in JS for one-shots only: `bearingOf(ear: InRoom, yaw, source: InRoom)` → `{ rightward ∈ [-1, 1], roomsAway }`, then `voicedOf` gives `loudness = loud / (1 + (roomsAway / 0.6)²)` (half level at `HALF_AWAY = 0.6` room sides, i.e. 3 m on floor 1), `balance = rightward * clamp01(roomsAway / 0.3)` (stereo pan), `reverb = clamp01(0.35 + 0.5 * roomsAway)`. These are fixed when the one-shot starts; a sound does not move while it plays.
- `hearing.body(cues, pose)` is the only per-frame feed of the pose (it uses `pose.n` and `pose.scale`); `hearing.rooms(heard, pose, nowMs)` plays each happening's sound once (keyed `room/cue/sinceEpoch`, remembered 60 s), only for rooms on the eye's floor, at `{ u: room.x - pose.x + source[0], v: room.y - pose.y + source[2] }`, offset by the seconds since it began.
- **Reverb applies** to everything through the current place's convolver: cues send `loudness * reverb` to `place.send`; `scape.play` (the voice) defaults to `reverb: 0`, dry.

What a surf/wind/hum bed therefore needs (none of it exists):

- **Outdoors as a place**: `setPlace` with a `Sound` of `hum: 0`, low `echo` (0.08 s floor) gives open air with only the duct-like `air` loop — it is band-passed at 320 Hz and sounds like ventilation, not wind. Surf and wind need new loop synths in `scape/model/tone.ts` (149 lines; follow `air`: render `length + fold`, fold the tail over the head at equal power) and a way to name them — either new fields on `Sound` (touches every theme's `sound` literal unless optional, and `sameTone`) or a second, separate bed API on the soundscape.
- **A positioned, moving-listener hum**: needs a new `Soundscape` method (e.g. a bed handle with `set({ loudness, balance })` driving a `GainNode` + `StereoPannerNode` per frame via `setTargetAtTime`), fed each frame from `hearing.ts` with `bearingOf(pose, pose.yaw, cubeAt)` and the same distance law. `soundscape.ts` is 259 lines; `Rig` holds `{ context, master, place }`. The existing `hum(hz, seed, rate)` synth is reusable as the cube's hum as it stands.
- The intro island is not a `Room` on the grid; `bearingOf` only needs two `InRoom`-shaped points in one frame, so it works with any shared 2-D frame, but `hearing.body` calls `themeOf(pose.n)` — an outdoor pose needs its own branch for the place's `Sound` and footing (`'soft'` and `'wet'` exist in `FOOTINGS`; sand would be `'soft'`).
- Tests that pin the bed: `scape/model/tone.test.ts` (hum pitch, seamless loops, impulse energy and decay). **`soundscape.ts` has no test** (browser-only); `hearing.ts` has none.

### (c) Voice

- `features/voice` is a **formant speech synthesiser in code**: `lineFor(moment, n, grain): Line` picks what is heard (`model/line-for.ts`), `voiceOf(who, n): Voice` picks the timbre (`model/voice.ts`), `utter(line, voice, rate): Float32Array` renders it (`model/utter.ts`, five resonators + a hiss band). `hearing.ts` plays it with `scape.play(samples, 32_000)` — full level, centred, **dry** (no reverb share passed).
- Three speakers (`SPEAKERS`): `visitor` — the protagonist, **who makes sounds and says no words**, one breathy throat (124→108 Hz) wearier with each floor; `building` — an announcer over loudspeakers (`piped`, with a chime), English lines in `SCRIPT.announcement`; `somewhere` — an unseen child/woman/man (`hello`, `numbers`, `laugh`, `lullaby`). Past floor 49 none is a throat. **Only the visitor is wired into the game**: `building` and `somewhere` are reached only by `scripts/voice-cube.ts` (which writes WAVs for listening). There is no PA voice in play today.
- `Moment` (`model/script.ts`, `MOMENTS`): `first-shrink`, `second-shrink`, `shrink`, `landing`, `mutter`, `announcement`, `numbers`, `hello`, `laugh`, `lullaby`. The visitor's are in `model/vocal.ts` as `Vocal` functions returning `{ text, phones }` — phones with `note`/`slide` (semitones), `beats`, `loud`, `ease`: `huh` (first shrink: "huh? huh?!"), **`groan` ("ugh…", already the second shrink's line and one of `SHRINKS`)**, `sigh`, `exhale`, `hm`, `mm`, `asked`, `pff`, `tsk`, `heh`, `oof`, `hup`, `mf`, `ah`, `hummed`. `LANDINGS = [oof, hup, mf, ah, exhale]`.
- Path of a `say`: `play.ts` emits `{ cue: 'say', moment: ShrinkMoment | 'landing' }` (`ShrinkMoment = 'first-shrink' | 'shrink'` in `features/player/model/look.ts`; `'landing'` is emitted with the landing cue at play.ts:186). `hearing.body` turns `'shrink'` into `'second-shrink'` when the previous said shrink was the first, then `lineFor(moment, n, room grain)` → `utter(…, voiceOf('visitor', n), 32000)` → `scape.play`.
- **A wordless groan belongs in voice, not in sound cues**: it is the visitor's throat, and `groan` already exists. To say it at a new moment (waking on the beach): add a member to `MOMENTS` and an entry in `LINES` (`line-for.ts`, a `Record<Moment, …>` so the compiler demands it), e.g. `wake: (_, dice) => vocal(groan, dice)` (export `groan` or add a `wake` export in `vocal.ts`); widen the player's `say` moment type; nothing in `hearing.ts` changes except that `afterFirst` logic treats any moment other than `'landing'` as resetting it (line 83) — a new non-shrink moment must be excluded there or the "second shrink" line is lost. A **swallow** has no phone for it (it is not a vowel, hum or fricative) and belongs in `features/sound/cues/model/body.ts`.
- Tests: `model/line-for.test.ts` ("says something at every moment on every floor" iterates `MOMENTS`; "gives the visitor sounds and no words, the same on every floor"; "wearies of shrinking"), `utter.test.ts` (323 lines), `lexicon.test.ts`, `filter.test.ts`.
