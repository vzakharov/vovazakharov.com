What you own and deliver:

- **Open water**: a wave field from a directional spectrum, synthesised on the GPU (FFT in compute, a few cascades, is the planned route; `core` will report whether the container's software WebGPU runs compute — the operator's machine does), displacing a mesh that reaches the horizon under a 20 km far plane. **Periodic in the hour** — dispersion frequencies quantised to multiples of 2π/3600 — and seeded, so the same water for everyone at the same moment. Whitecap foam must be a function of time, not of accumulated frame history.
- **Shading**: Fresnel against the sky map (`skyRadiance(dir, lod)`, rougher lods for sub-pixel slopes far away, so the horizon neither sparkles nor goes flat), the sun's glitter with slope statistics that stay right at distance, light scattered through crests, the colour of depth, foam that looks like foam, `aerial(…)` on distant water so the horizon melts into the sky, and the cube's shadow where `cubeShadow(p)` says. Target 0's alpha is metres from the eye; nothing blends in the scene pass.
- **The bed through shallow water, computed, not blended**: under a refracted ray, `islandHeight(xz)` gives the depth and the sand's colour comes from a function the `sand` package will export (ask me for its name once `sand` reports; until then a plain sand colour) — absorption by depth, a hint of caustics.
- **The shore**: waves that feel the bottom (shoaling, turning toward the beach, steepening, breaking into lines of white), and the **swash** — a thin sheet that runs up the beach, slows, stops and drains back, with a lace of foam at its edge. This is `waterLevel(xz, t)`, whose body is yours, with an exact TS twin: the sand's wet band, the surf's sound and the water on the player's legs all read it, so its timing is the scene's heartbeat — a wave every 7–10 s on the south-west beach where the player wakes, swell arriving from the south-west.
