# The outside: the contract every package builds to

The lead's design for the cube's intro, for every child session. Read this, then `facts-render.md`, `facts-play.md`, `facts-island-board.md`, `facts-frames-gates.md` and `facts-sound-floor1.md` in this directory as your package needs them (the last two may still be landing — `git fetch origin claude/cube-intro-j2wt34` and look again). The plan, with the operator's brief verbatim, is `docs/plans/cube-intro.in-progress.md`; only the lead edits it. A decision here that turns out wrong against the code is reported to the lead with what you measured, not worked around in private.

## What is being built

The player wakes lying on the shore of a tiny sand island at golden hour, feet in the swash, looking out to sea, the low sun behind them. They get up, turn round, and see the cube: 5 × 5 × 5 m, hanging about a metre over the sand, black with grey bands whose heights halve downward (the floors' own slabs), an open doorway in the top band and an ordinary ladder leaning to its sill. They can walk the island and swim out. That nothing holds the cube up is plain to see from anywhere on the beach — lit sand and the sea's glare under it — so no scene is spent on showing it (the operator, on the first frames: «катсцены "заглянуть под куб" не надо, и так отчётливо видно что он парит»). At the ladder, E climbs up and through the doorway into floor 1's room, which is lit by that same sun through the doorway and nothing else. From there the game is the game, except that every lever is now a pill on a small shelf.

The bar, in the operator's words: «он должен быть отрисован супер-пупер, прям супер-пупер, не отличишь от настоящего. И вода, с прибоем и шумом, и солнце и облака в небе -- всё, разумеется, процедурно-детерминированно». The first run's verdict on a first attempt was «это на пару порядков лучше чем было но это не уровень AAA». Look at your own frames and iterate on what you see; a package is not done when it compiles.

## Rules that hold for every package

- **Determinism, in place and in time**: two people at the same point at the same moment see and hear the same thing — the operator: «цель -- любая вещь, любое явление должно выглядеть абсолютно идентично в один и тот же момент времени для двух игроков на разных машинах в одной точке». So a frame is a pure function of the eye and the shared clock. No `Math.random`, no frame-count state that changes the picture, no input-dependent noise, and **nothing that remembers earlier frames**: no foam or wetness accumulated in a texture from frame to frame, no cloud or reflection history reprojected, no simulation stepped from when the page opened — a player who arrived a second ago sees what one who stood there an hour sees. What looks like memory (foam trailing a wave, sand staying wet behind the swash) is computed from the clock, by asking where the wave was. Only what the player's own body does is the player's own (their pose, their hands, a pill they took).
- **Interactions come at a price** — the operator: «любое, что меняет состояние мира силами одного игрока (вплоть до "пнуть мячик на полу") -- только покупкой». Nothing the player does leaves a mark on the world: no footprints, no wake or ripples from their body in the water, no sand kicked up that stays, no object moved. The world acts on the player (waves on their legs, sun on their hands); the player does not act on the world. The pill is the one thing taken, and the shelf holds a pill for whoever comes next: the one in the hand is the taker's own — the operator's reading of why: «таблетки на самом деле нет, это твоё собственное воображение, представляющее необходимость принять решение как физический, экстернальный объект и таким образом снимающее с себя ответственность». `src/cube/CLAUDE.md` holds this rule and the two above for every later session. **Every random number is `rolled`** — the operator: «всё что касается случайности должно использовать наш `rolled`». That is `roll` / `rollWith` from `@/dice/shared/roll`, under a named path, the way `features/decor` and `entities/world` draw theirs: a wave's phase, a dune's place, a cloud field's seed, a wobble in a scene's timing, a sound's grain are rolled in TS and interpolated into WGSL or passed as uniforms, never produced by a hand-made hash, a literal "random-looking" constant or an LCG of your own. The one thing a shader keeps for itself is per-pixel noise (grain, sparkle, a value-noise lattice), and that goes through the kit's hash pieces in `src/cube/entities/theme/kit/lib/hash.ts`, salted by a rolled value, not through a new hash function.
- **Every motion is periodic in the hour.** The shared clock gives `seconds` into the hour (0–3600) and `hour`. Anything that moves — waves, swash, clouds, hum — is a function of `seconds` that returns to itself at 3600, so nothing jumps on the hour and nothing needs a large float. A wave spectrum's frequencies are quantised to multiples of 2π/3600; a cloud field drifts a whole number of its tiles per hour.
- **Everything procedural, and no textures**: no image, mesh or audio asset — and nothing drawn is looked up from a stored image either, baked or computed. The operator: «никаких текстур. все стены, полы, потолки этажей куба отрисованы процедурно, здесь мы делаем то же». Every surface is a procedure evaluated in the shader from place and clock, as the floors' walls are. So: no FFT displacement or normal maps, no sky map, no noise texture, no lookup table, no baked field. The only textures are the frame's own screen-space targets — the HDR scene targets and what the reflection, bloom and lens passes make of them — and a layer may add one of that kind (a reduced-resolution target of the current frame, composited the same frame), never one parameterised by world position or direction.
- **No outside services, no new runtime dependency** without the lead's word.
- **The repo's conventions are not negotiable**: MASD (`.claude/rules/masd.md`), the linters, no lint-suppression comments, no `interface`, `pnpm type-overlap`, kebab-case, and **no text file over 400 lines** — split at a seam before you reach it. Several files you may touch are already near it (`cube-canvas.tsx` 362, `geometry.ts` 346, `hands.ts` 327, `play.ts` 313, `renderer.ts` 297, `cube-scene.ts` 270). «Чистый, красивый, сухой код».
- **Tests**: anything that is a pure function of its input gets a `node:test` beside it (`.claude/rules/testing.md`). A WGSL function with a TS twin gets a test that the twin is what the WGSL says where that is checkable.
- **Never touch `main`, open no PR, publish no preview, ask the operator nothing.** Work on your own branch, push every step that passes, `wip:`-commit every few files (a container can restart without warning). Commit subjects use the repo's prefixes.
- **Before you report**: quick gates green for what you touched (`facts-frames-gates.md`), `/dry` and `/tend-prose` run over your own diff, frames of your work under `docs/remove-before-merging/cube-intro-j2wt34/frames/<package>/` committed (a handful, `.webp` or `.png`, each under 300 KB) with one line each on what it shows.
- **Report** with `mcp__claude-code-remote__send_message` to `session_01DaohkEr63nSs6fbnbbEpGd`: your branch and head, what is built and wired, what is left, what you need changed outside your files, each place you departed from this contract and why. Then end your turn; the lead's next message wakes you. Report early as well as at the end: the first time you have a frame worth looking at, say so.

## The world frame

- **W, in metres**: x east, y up, z south, right-handed; still sea level is y = 0. The cube's own frame C (`entities/world/model/frame.ts`) is floor 1's room sides with its origin at the north-west corner of floor 1's room on its slab, so `W = CUBE_AT + 5 · C`, no rotation. In C the cube is x, z ∈ [0, 1], y ∈ [−0.5, 0.5].
- **"East" is the frame's word, not the world's.** The sun sets where the doorway looks.
- **The doorway** replaces the decor door of floor 1's room, where the planted world puts it: in the east wall (x = 5 m in the room), z from 0.274 to 1.244 m — by the north-east corner — sill at the slab, 2.1 m tall. The potted plant stands in that corner, half across the opening, and stays.
- **The sun does not move**: elevation 9°, azimuth +x turned 31° toward −z, so `SUN_DIR` (unit, toward the sun) ≈ (0.847, 0.156, −0.509). The azimuth is the operator's, from his sketch of the beam over floor 1's room (`frames/operator/sun-direction.webp`: «направление света солнца какое-то вот такое, наверное, должно быть», and of its steepness «я тут в основном про направление в двумерной плоскости говорил»): across the floor, the beam runs on a diagonal from the doorway toward the room's far south-west. 31° is the line from the pill's shelf (west wall, z = 3.75 m, 1 m up) back through the doorway's middle. The elevation is the lead's: at 9° the doorway's light lies along the whole diagonal of the floor and stands on the west wall from its foot to about 1.18 m, so the beam ends on the pill. `SUN_DIR` is one TS constant, built from the two named angles and interpolated into every shader that needs it; `shell` may tune either by a degree or two, nobody else.
- **The cube hovers with its underside 1.1 m over the sand beneath it**, above the island's crest. At 9° its shadow starts about 7 m from it and runs some 38 m west-south-west (31° south of west), so the sand under it is sunlit.
- **The staging**: the ladder stands on the sand east of the cube, at the doorway. The player wakes about 30 m to the cube's south-west, at the waterline, feet seaward, the sun behind them; turning, they see the cube against the sun, which stands low to the right of it, level with its upper half, its shadowed south and west faces toward them, its long shadow lying on the beach a few metres to their north, the doorway and ladder out of sight — the operator's steer: «солнце наверное лучше всё-таки сзади или сбоку от куба, если смотреть на него с места "просыпания", потому что иначе ты сразу видишь дверь и лестницу in your face». The cube's long shadow lies on the sand beside the way to it. The island is small — some 90 m east to west, 50 m across, the crest about 1.6 m over the sea, a broad gentle beach on the south-west where the player wakes, a shorter one east of the cube under the ladder.
- **The inside keeps its look.** The room, the hands and the body are stylised on purpose; only the outside is photoreal — the operator: «кривые руки -- украшать не надо; контраст между ослепительным реализмом предазкатного острова и недореализмом остального -- будем считать творческим выбором».
- **The machine** is an M2 Pro, 16 GB, in Chrome, where the game today runs near 111 fps. The outside holds 60 fps there at full canvas size; a render scale that adapts is the fallback, not the plan.

## One renderer, one HDR target

The cube's renderer (`src/cube/pages/cube/ui/renderer.ts`) stays the only one. The outside joins its scene pass; `facts-render.md` § 4 has the constraints, of which these bind everyone:

- Three colour targets, all `rgba16float`, no blending, no MSAA; depth `depth32float`, reversed-Z (`depthCompare: 'greater'`), not readable.
- **Target 0's alpha is metres from the eye**, not opacity; `1e4` means sky. Target 1's alpha is mirror strength: write 0 unless you mean to be a screen-space mirror.
- Nothing translucent in the scene pass. Water shows its bed by computing it (below), not by blending over it.
- Draw order in the scene pass: sky backdrop, sand, the cube's shell and ladder, the rooms and what moves in them, the sea.
- The far plane outside is 20 km; sight (`FAR` = 24 room sides) stays what `sight()` uses for rooms. Reversed-Z in float keeps the precision.

**Light, in the scene's own linear units**, where floor 1's lamps are 1.5 and bloom starts at 1.0:

- A white Lambert face square to the sun shows `SUN_LIGHT` ≈ 8 × (1.0, 0.62, 0.33). Flat ground gets `sin 9°` ≈ 0.156 of that.
- The sky lights a white upward face to about 2.6, bluer than the sun. `SKY_FILL` and a ground-bounce term come from the sky package; until then the core's placeholders.
- So dry sand on the flat sits near 1.5–2, a sunlit wall inside the room near 4 and blooming, the sun's disc in the hundreds. The outside's own lens exposes for that; floor 1's lens (exposure 1) sees the doorway overexposed, as an eye in a dim room would.

## The seam (package `core`)

`core` lands the pieces two or more packages need, with their final signatures and placeholder bodies, so everyone else builds against them from the first hour:

- **Where the eye is**: a union the page holds — inside (`Pose`, as today) or outside (`{ eye: Vec3 in W, yaw, elevation }`). `cube-scene.ts` renders an outside eye with floor 1's room as the fixed mesh frame, so the real room shows through the doorway. The `?play` hook gains a way to set an outside eye, so any package can shoot an outside frame headlessly.
- **`OutdoorFrame` uniforms**, one buffer bound as group 0 for outdoor pipelines: `viewProjection` (W → clip), its inverse, `eye` (W), `sun` (`SUN_DIR`), `clock` (seconds, hour, canvas size).
- **A layer shape** every outdoor drawer has: made from the device and the shared layouts, an optional `prepare(encoder, frame)` for a screen-space pass of its own before the scene pass (clouds at reduced resolution, say — never a map of the world or the sky), `draw(pass)` inside it, `dispose()`. The renderer holds the layers in the order above.
- **WGSL pieces with TS twins**, each one a single source interpolated both ways where it can be:
  - `islandHeight(xz) -> f32` — metres over still sea level, negative under water. Body owned by `sand`.
  - `waterLevel(xz, t) -> f32` — the sea's slow surface: still level plus surge and swash, without wind waves. `waterLevel > islandHeight` means under water now. Body owned by `sea`. The sand's wetness, the surf's sound and the waves on the player's feet all read this one function, which is what keeps them in step.
  - `cubeShadow(p) -> f32` — 1 in sun, 0 in the shadow of the cube and the ladder, soft with distance. Body owned by `shell`.
  - `skyRadiance(dir, lod)` — the sky's radiance in a direction, as a pure function of direction and clock, for the backdrop, the sea's reflections and fill light. `lod` trades detail for cost (0 the full sky with clouds as the backdrop draws them; higher values a cheaper, blurrier sky for a rough reflection or for fill). No map behind it. Body owned by `sky`.
- **The outside lens**: tone mapping and exposure for an eye outdoors, since lenses are per floor today.
- **Where the code lives.** The maths and WGSL of sky, sea and sand import nothing of the cube's but `shared` — only `kernel`, `scene`, `dice` beyond that — because the island game (`src/island`) is to take them later; `core` settles the slice names with the MASD checkers, and says so in its first report.

## Packages

Each is a child session on `claude/cube-intro-<name>`, cut from the lead's branch; merge `origin/claude/cube-intro-core` as soon as it has the seam, and the lead's branch whenever told.

- **core** — the seam above.
- **sky** — atmosphere at a 9° sun, the sun's disc and glow, clouds that look like clouds at sunset, all as functions of direction and clock; `skyRadiance`; `SUN_LIGHT`, `SKY_FILL` as numbers the model gives.
- **sea** — open water to the horizon (a rolled spectrum summed analytically — trochoidal waves displaced in the vertex stage, finer ones in the normal, each faded out before it aliases — glitter path under the sun, foam, the colour of depth), the shore (shoaling, breaking, swash running up the beach and draining back), the bed seen through shallow water by computing `islandHeight` and the sand's colour under a refracted ray; `waterLevel`.
- **sand** — the island's shape, the sand (grain, ripples, the sparkle of low sun, damp and wet bands by `waterLevel`'s recent reach), the cube's and ladder's long shadows on it, footprints if they come cheap; `islandHeight`.
- **shell** — the cube from outside (the bands, a material that is not of this world and plays in the low sun as far as black and grey allow), the doorway cut through floor 1's wall with its reveal, the ladder, the sunbeam inside floor 1 falling on floor, walls, bench and plant, the ceiling panels off; `cubeShadow`.
- **walk** — moving outside (terrain underfoot, wading, swimming with no edge), where a session starts, the scripted scenes (waking, the climb, and the climb back down — the look under the cube is cut), the body seen from its own eyes. A bare `/cube` wakes on the beach; the address fragment is first written when the body crosses the doorway inward, so a refresh before that plays the intro again and one after it starts inside (the operator: «урл сменяется на наш стандартный "запоминающий при обновлении страницы хеш" (честный, не cheated) при переходе дверного проёма»); `?cheated_to=` and `?play` start inside as today. Nothing is remembered between page loads.
- **pill** — the lever becomes a pill on a shelf at the lever's place, on every floor; hands that can hold it; take, to the mouth, swallow, shrink. On floor 1 only, every game: the hand lifts the pill and turns it, looking, and the page asks "Press Y to drink, N to put down"; Y finishes the swallow, N puts it back. The game is English only.
- **sound** — surf timed by `waterLevel`, wind, the cube's hum placed in space, a groan on waking, the ladder, the swallow, footsteps on sand and in water.
- **board** — the coordinator for the design board (running).
