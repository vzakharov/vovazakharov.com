# Frames from `core`: the seam with stand-in layers

Shot with `pnpm outside:cube --out "$PWD/tmp/cube-outside"`, 1280×800,
SwiftShader.

- `wake-sea.webp` — the waking spot, propped on an elbow, looking out to sea:
  flat sea shaded by the sky map, the island's shoulder on the right.
- `wake-cube.webp` — the same spot turned to the cube: sand over `islandHeight`,
  the shell's six dark faces, the sun's disc off to the right.
- `under.webp` — on the sand under the cube, looking up: the shell's underside
  fills the frame, black.
- `ladder-foot.webp` — where the ladder will stand, looking up the east face:
  sunlit, no doorway cut and no ladder.
- `doorway-out.webp` — floor 1's room at the doorway's place, looking east: the
  room's wall, the doorway uncut.
- `room-doorway.webp` — floor 1's room looking at the doorway's place: the room
  as it is today, through floor 1's own lens.
- `wake-cube-eyelids-045.webp` — `wake-cube` with `--eyelids 0.45`: the outside
  lens's lids half shut.
