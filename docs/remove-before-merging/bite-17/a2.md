# Package a2 — hand-over note

"Left — designed" items 2 and 3 of `a-light.md`. Paths under
`src/pages/mushrooms/ui/scene/`.

## Done

- Clouds: `paintClouds(layer, { dusk, rain }, layout, random)` draws each
  cloud, then its dusk twin (same puffs in `DUSK`'s cloud colours, a high
  one hazed toward `DUSK.skyTop`), then its rain twin, so the rain's lies
  over the dusk's. `Backdrop.duskClouds` holds them; `placeClouds` and
  rain-view's `scaleCloud` move them with the cloud. `twin-fade.ts` holds
  `shade` (was rain-view's) and `focusTwins` (rain-view's filter-camera
  setup); rain-view and `paintBackdrop` both import them.
- Hills and brow: `drawHills(layers, hills, view, tones)` and
  `drawBrow(graphics, blades, view, tones)`; a `Range` keeps `toned`, a key
  into `tones.ranges`. `BROW` is no longer exported from `backdrop-tones.ts`.
- **One entry point for the light: `backdrop.relight(level)`**, replacing
  a1's `showDusk`. It sets the dusk pictures' alpha, fades the dusk cloud
  twins whole (`shade`), and redraws the hills and the brow in
  `tonesAt(level)` when the level, quantised to 1/64 (`LIGHT_STEPS`), or the
  heading changes (`hillsFrom: { heading, level }`). `Backdrop.level` is
  kept over a repaint, and `paintBackdrop` relights at it, so a repaint at
  dusk stays dusk. The dusk twins' filter cameras are focused in
  `paintBackdrop` itself (the scene's camera zoom is set before it runs).

- Day unchanged: the `opening` play on tabL (its four frames, the turned
  one included) is pixel-identical to the same play at the fork point 41188392. `frames/bite-17/a2-day.png`, and `a2-dusk.png` /
  `a2-dusk-turned.png` from an uncommitted edit opening `level` at 1: dusk
  sky, stars, lavender clouds with rose undersides, dusk hills and brow
  meeting the dusk ground with no seam; the sun still shows (DuskView's).

## Decided

- `relight` does all three (pictures, cloud twins, hills) rather than the
  DuskView fading the twins itself, so the backdrop's light has one seam.
- The pictures and cloud twins follow the exact level; only the live-drawn
  hills and brow step at 1/64, the step too small to see against the
  cross-faded ground bake.
- A shower at dusk shows the rain twin (day's slate colours) over the dusk
  twin, as the order of creation stacks them.
