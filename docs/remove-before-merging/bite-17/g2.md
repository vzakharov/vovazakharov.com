# Package g2 — the brow's grass walks with the meadow, and dims at dusk

Done; landed as one commit on the shared branch.

## Frames (tabL, crops of the brow's band, `frames/bite-17/g2-*.png`)

- `g2-opening` vs `g2-before-walked`: before, the tufts under the brow sit at
  the same pixels after 4 units walked. `g2-after-walked`: they moved.
- `g2-before-dusk` vs `g2-after-dusk`: the light-green tufts at full dusk are
  gone; the grass now sits a shade off the dusk ground.
- The dusk play's frame budget went 12.7 → 15.7 ms median (within 26 ms):
  the band's tufts cost a distance check each frame and more tufts drawn.
  Left: if dusk perf needs it, prefilter the seam by cell (only cells the
  band crosses) instead of every live seam tuft.

## Cause

The light tufts just under the brow are the seam's grass (`seamGrass`), which
was fixed to the horizon: each tuft held an azimuth and a row under the brow,
so a turn slid it but a step never moved it, while the lawn's tufts and
mottles round it moved with the ground. Seen in the `walk` play on tabL: the
opening and `walk-forward` frames (4 units walked) show the same tufts under
the brow at the same pixels, every lawn tuft below them moved. At dusk they
also kept their day colours (`tuftColours` read only the day's), lighter than
the darkened ground, so the standing-still showed.

## Fix

- The seam's grass now grows on the plane, `SEAM_PER_CELL` tufts per lawn
  cell drawn after its tufts and mottles (`cellLawn`, so the lawn's own draws
  are unchanged), and is drawn through the view as the lawn's are
  (`shownSprouts`), only in the band `SEAM_BAND` deep under the brow and past
  it, sinking (`shownSeam`). A step brings each nearer and lower, at the
  parallax of its distance; the band's nearer half fades a tuft into the
  ground (`seamFaded`), so none goes in one frame.
- The grass is toned in the light of the frame's duskness (`turfAt`): the
  ground under it from `tonesAt`, its own colours between `PALETTE` and their
  `DUSK` twins. `DUSK.tuft` is darkened (0x629260 → 0x5a8858) so a blade
  stands over the dusk ground about as far as by day over the day's
  (`backdrop-tones.test.ts`). `Grass.update` takes the duskness.
- `Grass.paint` no longer takes the backdrop's stream: nothing of the grass is
  drawn from it now (it was its last consumer, so nothing else shifts).

## Decided

- `SEAM_BAND` is one clump-size deep (about the old 9 % of the ground's depth
  on tabL, 12.5–13.3 units out) and `SEAM_PER_CELL` 2.2 a square clump-size,
  about the old count on screen.
- `DUSK.tuft` also tones the brow's lit blade tips (`tonesOf`'s `bladeLit`),
  which dim a little with it.
