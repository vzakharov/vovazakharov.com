## Intent

Вова is building the cube's for-sale economy as a deliberate joke: shop-style
prices, a $10M room on floor 2, every floor an eighth of the one above, and a
flat $4.99 from floor 9 down. Payment is still being designed; he is thinking.

## Decisions

- **Tag rule**: the nearest price ending in nines, above or below — beat "the
  largest not above" because Вова wanted $38.15 → $39.99 («психологичнее»).
- **$4.99 floor**: a minimum on `priceOf`, not a bulk lot — Вова dropped
  «оптом».
- **Real price in the economy table** only for floors > 9.
- **Paying (open)**: Stripe takes $0.50–$999,999.99 a charge, so floors 2–3 for
  bitcoin (an address and a sum on a static page); one payment link per floor
  below, quantity left to the buyer.
