Iteration zero of the shrinking-cube game at `/cube`, per the plan. Bite 3 is

routed, holes visible both ways, a hue per floor, timelapse frames. Inside it,

## 4. Decisions

- **Seals belong to dice.** `sealWith(world, keys)` in
  `src/dice/shared/roll/seal.ts`; the cube's `seals(seed).hole.at({n,x,y})` has
  the preimage `<seed>/hole/<n>/<x>/<y>`, frozen byte for byte by `world.json`.
  Any change replants the world.
- **67 floors.** Floor 67 throws `RangeError`; that crash is the ending.
- **`dice` waits for its second child** (the island) through
  `WAITING_APPS` in `eslint/masd-graph.ts`, on Вова's yes.
- **Graffiti economy** (board zone "Graffiti economy", all ideas, nothing to
  build before release): start price $10k at floor 1's start room, ×⅓ a floor,
  never below cost; price follows the path; a paid wall is forever at a high
  price or honest rent, never painted over; ruled out — per-floor constant,
  one-off auction, overpaint ×2, payouts or transferable credit or gifts,
  measuring passers, a Worker hole check. Argon2 is "just an idea"; if adopted,
  the difficulty-calculator artifact must follow.
