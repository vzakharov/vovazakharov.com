# S4 — the flier checks learn the shelter

## Done

- `scripts/lib/flier-watch.ts`: `worstRest` judges a flier settled under a
  cap as it judges one on a flower or a cap top.
- `ui/scene/visit-play.ts`: `Playing.rains`, a cloud tapped on the first tick
  at or after each.
- `ui/scene/fliers.test.ts` `describe('a shower')`: tablet and phone, clump
  and forest, the first three seeds, a 30 s visit with rain at 10 s (~2 s for
  all four cases; run alone with `--test-name-pattern="a shower"`). Breaks
  on: a flight before the tap other than the dry visit's; one leaving; two
  crowded (`seatedClash`, shelters included); while it rains, a leg to
  anything but a shelter or the air, or one coming out; 4 s after the tap,
  under half of the most that ever sheltered together sitting in one; after
  the stop, one coming out on any tick but the first at or after its own
  `lingerOf`, or one still under a cap past the longest linger.
- Game fix, `model/insects.ts` `isDue`: a flier under a cap never gives way
  to a waiting bee (`givesWay`). Without it, at the stop a sheltering
  butterfly whose seat crowds a flower bolted out at once for a bee hovering
  in the air (phone, forest, seed 3), against call 10.

## Measured (before the check was written)

- Clump: 4 seats, 3 to 4 filled together; the rest roam the air (call 5).
  Forest: 8–10 of 10 under cover 2 s after the tap.
- A butterfly whose nearest open seat is far takes long to get there (6–8 s
  for 13–16 sizes at the sheltering 1.9 sizes/s), so call 9's "~2 s" holds
  for a near seat only. The check asks for most, not all, at 4 s.
