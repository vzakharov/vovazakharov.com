## The watch over a shower in the browser (measured, not fixed)

Measured with a scratch play (not committed): 6 mushrooms, all ten
released, 600 frames, a cloud tapped, 1020 frames on; the same play dry as
the control. tabL and phoneP.

- **Red at the dash, tabL:** `worstTurn` butterfly-1 11.35 rad/s against
  `MOST_TURN_RATE` 10.81, at 18167 ms, 167 ms after the rain started at 18000. Its leg: cap mushroom-4 → shelter mushroom-4 seat 1 (from the top of
  a cap to under the same cap), departs 18017, arrives 19332, sheltering dash,
  `lifted` −2.74 rad. phoneP hit 10.81 exactly at 18183 (passes by
  rounding). Dry control: worst turn a fly at 36.00 of 36.04; heading, spin,
  rest and light all inside their bounds both ways (heading 0 frames off;
  rest 0.45 of 0.57; spin ≤ 0.8 turn).
- **Why:** the steering caps the body at `TURN_RATE` (10.8), but the drawn
  rotation adds the bend (`insect-drawn.ts` `airborne · bentTurn`, here
  +0.015 rad over the steering's turn), so a body already turning at the cap
  is drawn ~5 % past it. The sheltering pace halves the flight, so the
  take-off pivot (`PIVOT_SHARE · flight · |lifted| / π` ≈ 290 ms for 2.74
  rad) asks ≈ 9.5 rad/s — at the cap once the bend joins. Dry, a butterfly's
  pivot is twice as long and never reaches it.
- **Next step, game side (spec § 4 says lower the hurry before touching the
  watch):** either give the pivot a floor in time for a sheltering leg (e.g.
  pivot from the kind's dry `flying` low end, not the sheltering flight), or
  lower the butterfly's sheltering `flying` low end from 1200 toward ~1500
  and re-measure. Check by re-running the scratch play above: add
  `['zzshower', play]` to `PLAYS`, a play releasing all ten, stepping 600,
  tapping `__probe.clouds()`, stepping ~1000, reading `window.__watch`.
  S3's `play-rain.ts` frames with fliers released before the tap would run
  `checkWatch` over the same moment.
