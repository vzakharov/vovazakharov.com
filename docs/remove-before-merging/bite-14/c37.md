# C37 — a chase ends when the finger lifts, and any key cancels it (call 37)

## Done

- Step 1, the fix and its tests (`model/stride.ts`, `model/walk.ts`, their tests):
  - A lifted chase asks the cruise for rest (`toward` 0), so it eases to a
    stop over `KEY_EASE` from wherever it is, braking harder only where a
    nearer target would otherwise be overrun; it no longer runs on to its
    target.
  - A chase records its `axis` (`chaseFrom(stride, heading, axis)`, the
    bearing derived inside). A walking or strafing key going down
    (`holding`) and a turning key going down (`walk.ts` `holdTurn`) call
    `yieldChase`: the chase ends where the eye stands, lifted or not, and
    its pace carries on as that axis's key pace, so the keys ease from it
    without a jolt — a key the other way turns it back over `KEY_EASE`.
  - Keys already held when a press starts still wait for the chase (a held
    key repeating changes nothing, so it never cancels).
  - Three walk tests that lifted mid-chase and expected the target reached
    now hold the finger until the eye settles, then lift.

## Pan

`model/pan.ts` has no chase: a sky drag's turn follows the finger 1:1, and
after the lift it glides on by the finger's velocity — a deliberate fling,
not a run-on to a left-behind target. A turn key already takes a glide over
(`holdKey` hands the glide's pace to the keys). Unchanged.
