  strafing drag from `BARE_START`, its reckoning from `planeSeen` under the

## Left

- The walk play on tabL and phoneP, and a look at the strafe and turn frames.

## Decided

- A ground strafe's ground is the crossing's own (exact in x); its row
  drifts a few px as the azimuth widens (up to ~14 px on a small phone over
  150 px), since a strafe keeps depth, not range — vertical finger motion is
  not followed during a strafe anyway.
