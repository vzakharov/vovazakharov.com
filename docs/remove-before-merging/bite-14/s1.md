## Decided here (not in bite-14.md)

- **Shelter applies only when the sight carries `shelters`.** A sight without
  the field keeps dry habits in the rain. That is what makes step 1 change
  nothing on screen; once step 2's scene always sends `shelters` (maybe `[]`),
  call 2 holds in full (`[]` → everyone roams the air, call 5).
- A shelter leg's own stay is 0; `stayingDry` sets it. After the stop the
  sight still offers the seat (so `isDue` does not fire at `stopsAt`), but dry
  `nextPerch` never offers shelters and `settles` is limited to flower/cap.
- Shelter tests sit in `shelter.test.ts`, not `insects.test.ts`/`swarm.test.ts`.
