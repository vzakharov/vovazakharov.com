## Decided here

- Measured: `laidOf(...).opening ≈ CLUMP_DISTANCE` for every mushroom, so
  `place.size` against the layout's butterfly span is a fair, same-depth
  comparison. With it, tabL forest seeds 1–3 offer 9–10 of 10 domes, phoneP
  6 of 10; the opening clump always offers both.
- `place.size` does **not** carry a sprout's growth (P2 scales the bed's
  drawing, not the layout), so the sprout part of call 11 needs a clock in
  `perchSight`: the optional `now` above.
