# Song slugs — proposal

Rule (Vova): «берём английское название или перевод, но если слишком длинно, то сокращаем». Applied as: the English title or the `en` translation, lowercased, punctuation dropped, a leading “the”/“a” dropped, and anything past about four words cut to its most recognisable part. Edit a line's right-hand side in place; a line marked ❓ is a judgement call to confirm.

**Kept as they are**: the ten songs already live on `main` — `birdie`, `crossroads`, `first`, `june`, `letim`, `rak`, `reka-2`, `sashas`, `slime`, `wereback`. A static export has no redirects, so renaming them breaks every link already out there. Say if they should move anyway.
