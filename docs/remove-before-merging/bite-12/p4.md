## Decided

- **"Survive a reload" read as a relayout.** The game keeps nothing across
  a page reload (the visit seed is fresh each load; only the mute is in
  `localStorage`), so planted flowers do not survive one either. What they
  survive is a resize's relayout, by living in `Meadow`; `pulled` does the
  same for seeded flowers, whose ids are stable per visit.
- A replacement is a new planted flower plus the old id in `pulled`, not a
  reseeded old id: the bed's `reconcile` grows and sounds fresh ids, and a
  seeded flower has no record of its own to reseed.
- Pulling a parent keeps its bees' flowers where they stand.
