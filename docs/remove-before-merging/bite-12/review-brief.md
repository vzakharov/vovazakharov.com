result, and why it matters to a six-year-old or to the next bite. Rank by
that. Not a finding: style the linters own, taste, a decision restated,
anything already on `to-check.md`. Five strong findings beat fifteen thin
ones. Mark each `blocking` or `nit` in its first word.
