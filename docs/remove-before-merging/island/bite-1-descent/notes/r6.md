# R6 — a clean coastline

## Done

1. Smoothing (`entities/coast/model/smooth.ts`, exported `smoothCoast`,
   `SmoothOptions`, and `signedArea`): drop rings under `minArea`, resample each
   ring at `resampleStep`, Gaussian blur along the ring (`sigma`), D–P at
   `tolerance`; the blur halves per ring until the result keeps its turning, is
   simple and crosses no ring already smoothed. Largest first. Script runs it at
   `minArea 60, step 1, sigma 3, tolerance 0.3` px. 7 tests in
   `smooth.test.ts`.
2. `coast.json` regenerated: 25 rings, 1 356 vertices, 6.2109 km/px (was
   6.2009: the main body's bbox narrowed a little), 38 KB. **No ring dropped**:
   the tracer's own `islandFloor` (60 px²) already removes specks; the smallest
   kept ring is 239 px² (~9 200 km²) and every kept ring is an island inked on
   the drawing. Overlay regenerated in `frames/coast-overlay.png`.
3. `Simplified` (`{ tolerance }`) base type in `ring.ts`, shared by
   `TraceOptions` and `SmoothOptions` (type-overlap).

## Finding

At orbit the tiles are level 3: 2 048 km a side, 32 cells, so **64 km between
vertices**. Apatin, the northern fingers and Korump's arm are 10–20 km wide on
the plane; the grid samples them on and off, which is what frays them into
specks at orbit. Smoothing the polygon cannot fix that; the vertex density at
orbit is the terrain's (R7's paths).
