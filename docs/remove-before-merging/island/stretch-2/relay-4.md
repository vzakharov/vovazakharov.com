- **Flicker cause**: `coverOf` lets a held ancestor stand in for an unheld
  selected tile and hide the held fine tiles under it; reselects every 2–3
  frames on a turn (80°/s vs `RESELECT_TURN = cos 0.05`) and nearly every frame
  on a climb. Fix: draw the last fully-held selection (`shown`) until the new
  one is complete; evict keeps `selected ∪ shown`.
- **Relay watches** (6d1bff74): `/relay` Step 1 stops every artifact watch;
  `.claude/hooks/relay-watch-gate.py` (Stop) holds it; `board-notice.py` turns a
  stopped board watch back into a missing one on the operator's next prompt
  unless a successor was started. Belongs upstream in `vzakharov/muthur` too.
