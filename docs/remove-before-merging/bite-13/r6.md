# R6 — the bud: hand-over note

## Done

- **Step 1, a closed flower reads as a bud (call 13).** `flower-closing.ts`
  holds the bud: `BUD` (in shares of a ring's open petal length: foot 0.4
  below the head's middle, tip 0.95 above it — 1.35 of the 2.0 open head
  height, about two-thirds; outermost petals 0.28 out at the foot, leaning in
  0.3 rad; petals 1.35× as wide) and `petalPose`, which swings each petal
  from where it points open to standing up in the bud, the short way round,
  by the closing. `Folding` is now the closing plus the centre's share
  (gone under the petals by 1/1.6 shut, so a closed flower shows no dot) and
  the petals' width. `draw-flower.ts` paints each petal at its pose; past
  halfway shut it paints the lower petals in front and a two-ring head's
  inner ring behind the outer. Petal colour is the open one, plain.
- **Perches follow the bud:** `foldedHead` gives the rim (bee) closing to the
  bud's foot and the centre (butterfly) rising to its tip; the tap reach stays
  the open head's (R2's `hit`, untouched).
- Frames: `docs/remove-before-merging/frames/bite-13/tabL-r6-closed.png`,
  `phoneP-r6-closed.png`, and `r6-buds-open-half-closed.png` (three flowers,
  open / ~0.4 / closed, crops from tabL and phoneP). Shot through an
  uncommitted play dispatching `{kind:'rain', now: scene.clock * 1000}`.

## Decided

- `PetalPose` is a labelled tuple, the leading arguments of the petal
  outlines (`outline(...pose, width)`), rather than an object: an object with
  `foot`/`angle`/`length` overlaps `Footed`, `Mottle` and `Wing` member by
  member under `pnpm type-overlap`, and those modules are not this package's.
