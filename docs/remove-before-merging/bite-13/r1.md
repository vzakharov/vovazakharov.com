## Decided here (not in bite-13.md)

- Every cloud tap wobbles the tapped cloud (motion.ts `wobble`, volume kept),
  the first one included — rain.md asks it of a tap while raining.
- The darkening reuses `wetness`'s 1.5 s curve, shifted by the lag, rather
  than a second curve: the tapped cloud starts first, the farthest 0.6 s
  later.
- `lead` (the cloud that started the shower, which the others darken after)
  is kept apart from `tapped` (the cloud tapped last, which the drops fall
  densest under), so a restart from another cloud does not reshuffle the
  darkening.
- A shower started while the last one still dries does not drop the wash
  and the twins to 0 (the model's `wetness` does): the scene shows the wetter
  of the two spans. R2's flower closing and caps read `wetness` straight from
  the model, so they will still jump there; say if they should read
  `wetnessShown` instead.

## How R4 drives drops from here

`RainView.update` runs once a frame with the span. R4 adds a drops module
constructed in `RainView`'s constructor (depth `hudDepth - 1`, over the
wash), calls its `update(ms, downpour(showers.rain, ms), under)` from
`RainView.update`, where `under` is `this.placed()[this.tapped]` (the tapped
cloud on screen, or `undefined` when off it — then spread the drops across
the screen); its `paint(layout)` from `RainView.paint`; and a gush from
`RainView.tap` when `raining` was already true. `dropsInAir()` returns its
count. The view for `ofGround` is `backdrop.view` (the one the backdrop last
followed). R3's sound goes in the same three places.
