# X2 — review §§ 4–6 (cloud reach, rain out of the cloud, drops with no ground)

## Done

- **§ 4** — the puff layout's numbers live in `ui/scene/cloud-puffs.ts`
  (`PUFFS`); `paintClouds` reads them, and `PUFF_REACH` derives the drawn
  reach from them at its widest (any shaping, any sun): 2.78 r across,
  1.04 r above, 1.22 r below. `CLOUD_SPREAD` (`rain-sky.ts`) is
  `PUFF_REACH.across`, so `cloudSpan`, `placeClouds`' hide margin and the
  probe's `clouds` follow; `cloudAt` reaches up and down by the puffs, never
  short of `TAP_RADIUS`.

- **§ 5** — `shownFrom` (`rain-fall.ts`) gives how far above its landing a
  drop first shows: below the lowest showing cloud's box (`cloudBox`,
  `cloud-puffs.ts`) its slanted path passes behind, else all of its fall.
  `RainDrops` keeps each drop's start above the screen (so the lead-in
  stagger stands) and hides its streak till it is out from under the
  cloud: a drop whose column lies under a cloud's drawn span shows from its
  underside (y + 1.22 r), and none is ever drawn over a cloud. `RainView`
  hands it every placed cloud. Frame:
  `docs/remove-before-merging/frames/bite-13/review/x2-tabL-rain-from-cloud.png`.

## Left

- **§ 6** — measure `if (!foot) return true` in `rain-drops.ts`.

## Decided

- The reach is the puffs' widest, not their mean: a tap on any drawn puff
  must land, so a tightly shaped cloud (2.05 r) keeps up to ~0.7 r of slack.
- The underside is the widest reach below too, 1.22 r (shade layer sunk
  0.14 r plus the sun's 0.08 r lean), not the brief's ≈ 1.1 r: at 1.1 r a
  head could still show over a cloud's shaded underside when the sun stands
  above it. Toward a cloud's ends its puffs are smaller, so there rain shows
  up to ~0.4 r below the puffs.
- A drop "starts at the underside" by being hidden till it gets there, not
  by starting there: starting there would have every drop of a shower's
  first frames appear in one row under the cloud at once.
