# R2 — the meadow wets: hand-over note

## Done

- **Step 1, flowers close (call 6).** `flower-closing.ts` holds the closing:
  `closingStep` (wetness → one of 12 steps), `folding` (the head's shape
  that far shut: petal reach to 45 %, width to 80 %, centre disc to 40 %,
  petals starting at the middle), `closingsDue` (which heads a frame repaints:
  at most 6, drawn before hidden, nearest first) and `meanClosing`.
  `draw-flower.ts` paints a head `folded`; `flower-shown.ts` keeps each
  flower's painted `closing` and repaints its head alone (`closeShown`) in
  the light it was last painted in. `FlowerBed.update(t, rain, insects, held)`
  takes the span (`meadow?.rain`) and works out `wetness(rain, t * 1000)`
  itself, so `meadow-scene.ts` changed by one argument and no import.
- **Perches stay on the head:** `headR` and `disc` follow the folded head, so
  a bee hangs at the closed rim and a butterfly sits on the shrunken centre;
  the tap reach (`hit`) stays the open head's, so a closed flower plays as an
  open one does. The picker's ring follows `headR`, shrinking with the head.
- **Probe:** `FlowerBed.closing()` is the flowers' mean closing, for
  `__probe.rain()` (R1/R5 wire it: `__probe.scene.flowers.closing()`).
