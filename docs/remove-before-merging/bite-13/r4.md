## Done

  - `ui/scene/rain-fall.ts` (+ test): `cloudSpan`, `dropColumn` (half the
    drops in the tapped cloud's drawn span while it shows, the rest across
    the screen), `firstCrossing` (where a drop's path first crosses a cap

  - `ui/scene/rain-drops.ts`: `RainDrops` — a pool of at most 120 slots, each
    a streak image and a ring image from two textures baked once
    (`rain-streak`, `rain-ring`), at `HUD_DEPTH - 1`. A drop starts above the
    screen's top, slanted (`SLANT` 0.22), and falls at 1.5 screen heights a
    second to a landing picked at its start: a column (`dropColumn`), a row
    between the brow and the screen's foot (`planeSeen` → a foot on the
    plane), then the first drawn cap its path crosses (`drawnMushrooms` +
    the cap outline of `tapArea`, in the cap graphics' own frame). The
    landing is re-placed every frame (`ofGround` for a foot, the cap's world
    matrix for a cap point), so it stays put as the child turns and walks.
    A ring widens and fades over 0.3 s. 96 drops in a full downpour; a cloud
    tap while it rains adds a gush of 24 under that cloud.
  - `hit-areas.ts`: `drawnMushrooms(scene)` exported (the scan the flower
    hit test already used), so drops and taps find caps the same way.
  - `rain-view.ts`: builds `RainDrops`, drives it from `update`, gushes from
    `tap` on a restart, counts `dropsInAir()`; `sound.shower(downpour,
wetness)` every frame (from `hear`, before the backdrop check) and
    `sound.whoosh()` on every cloud tap. `RainView` takes `MeadowSound`
    (`Pick<…, 'shower' | 'whoosh'>`) as its last argument.
  - `palette-backdrop.ts`: `rainDrop`, `rainSplash`.

## Decided here (not in bite-13.md)

- The landing row is picked uniform in screen rows between the brow and the
  screen's foot, then put on the plane — not uniform in ground distance,
  which would crowd half the splashes into the thin band under the brow.
- Drops and splashes share one pool slot each, so the 120 bound covers both.
- Drop and ring size scale from 0.45 at the brow to 1 at the screen's foot
  (streak 4.5 %, ring 5 % of the screen height there).
- The gush (rain.md: "a gush of drops under it") is 24 drops, all in the
  tapped cloud's span; the steady downpour leaves those 24 of the 120 free.
- `meadow-scene.ts` is 451 lines: the constructor call no longer fits one
  line with the fifth argument.
