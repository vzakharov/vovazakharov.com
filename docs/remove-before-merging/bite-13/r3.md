## The calls R4 makes (from `rain-view.ts`)

- **Every frame**, rain or not: `sound.shower(downpour(rain, now), wetness(rain, now))`
  with `model/weather.ts`'s functions and the meadow's `now`. A dry frame
  (both 0) is a field check; a steady shower (both unchanged within 0.004) is
  two comparisons — no node and no param touched. The voice is built the
  first frame a shower is heard and let go the first frame both reach 0.
- **On a cloud tap** (start or restart, the same tap that dispatches `rain`):
  `sound.whoosh()`.

Nothing else: the mute, a hidden tab and the synth not yet started are all
handled inside `MeadowSound` (a shower is not queued for `start`; the next
frame asks again once the synth runs).

## Design

- Hiss: a 3 s looping `brownNoise` buffer under a 450 Hz lowpass, level
  `0.075 · wetness`. Fade in/out is `wetness`'s own 1.5 s ease (call 8),
  each level change smoothed with a 0.08 s time constant.
- Patter: three looping 2.7 s buffers of baked ticks (Hann-windowed sines,
  420–900 Hz gliding down a third, 7 a second each), under a 1600 Hz lowpass,
  layer k at `0.03 · wetness · clamp(3·downpour − k, 0, 1)` — the tick rate
  follows `downpour` with no node per tick. Buffers are made once per
  context.
- Whoosh: the same brown noise under a lowpass opening 160→480→140 Hz,
  enveloped 0.2 s up / 0.85 s, peak 0.3, over a soft sine sigh 95→130→80 Hz
  (peak 0.08), through `play` like every voice (queued before `start`,
  dropped while muted).

## Render (Chromium `OfflineAudioContext`, 48 kHz, master 0.8 + the compressor)

Loudest / mean 2048-sample Hann frame, dB of power, above each edge:

| --------------------- | ----------- | ----------- | ----------- | ----------- |
| note C5 (72)          | −21.5/−31.8 | −21.5/−31.8 | −29.3/−39.5 | −71.8/−81.7 |
| note C4 (60)          | −19.1/−29.0 | −26.2/−36.0 | −69.5/−79.0 | −90.2/−99.7 |
| shower, full          | −30.4/−34.6 | −33.6/−39.9 | −62.1/−65.1 | −77.7/−80.8 |
| shower, downpour 0.34 | −31.1/−35.1 | −35.0/−41.2 | −61.9/−65.1 | −78.1/−80.8 |
| hiss alone            | −31.5/−35.2 | −37.1/−41.7 | −61.5/−65.2 | −77.6/−80.7 |
| whoosh                | −20.4/−30.9 | −31.3/−42.7 | −57.2/−67.7 | −73.3/−83.6 |

A full shower above 300 Hz is ~12 dB under a C5's loudest; the whoosh ~10
dB under. Above 1 kHz the shower sits ~33 dB under the note: nothing harsh.
The render was an ad-hoc script (esbuild-bundled `rain-voice.ts` and
`noteVoice`, evaluated in `scripts/lib/cdp.ts`'s Chromium, FFT in-page); no
committed render pattern exists to extend, and `part-loudness.ts` cannot
model brown noise or baked buffers.
