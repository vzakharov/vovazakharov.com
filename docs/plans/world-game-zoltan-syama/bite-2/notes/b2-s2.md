# b2-s2 — S2, the tracer keeps the drawn lakes (call 9)

**Done.** `coast.json` regenerated: 30 rings, 25 land + 5 lakes (it had 25, no
lakes). Island tests 125/125 pass. The checks pass: tsc, eslint, masd, cycles,
type-overlap and prettier.

## The rule

The holes were lost in two places. `landMask` kept every pixel the sea did not
reach, so a lake was land in the mask. Then `traceCoast` dropped every ring with
negative area (`area >= islandFloor`).

- `model/lake.ts`, called by `landMask`: a 4-connected piece of land walled off
  by raw ink is a lake when it holds at least one pixel clear of ink (farther
  than `inkClearance`, 2 px) and under `minColouredShare` (0.1) of those clear
  pixels are painted. It adds no new option.
- `traceCoast` keeps every negative ring, since the mask has already judged it.
  `islandFloor` still applies to land.
- `smoothCoast` drops only land under `minArea` (`isSpeck`), so the 39 px² pool
  survives.

What the floor was catching, measured on the drawing (ink-walled pieces with
paint under 0.1):

- **Letter counters written over crayon**, e.g. 31 px at (576–589, 341–349) in
  «Акка-Карамка / Таситу». They are coloured, and every one of them is 2 px deep
  or less.
- **Paper slivers between two strokes**, e.g. 95 px at (452–491, 262–266): the
  gap between the coast and a border under «Порт-Лиано».

Every one of these is at most 2 px deep, while the five lakes are 3, 4, 4, 9 and
18 px deep. The margin is one pixel, at lake 1.

## Departure from bite 1

The sea strokes cut through the ink into the round pool below the
Блаблания–Фейсис strait, because bite 1 read the pool as the inlet's head. The
operator's lake 1 is that pool. The drawing has the two coastlines meet at y
239, with the pool's own ink loop starting at y 243. The stroke now ends at
`[698.5, 238]` and the radius-3 pool stroke is gone. The strait still opens to
about y 1205 km (it reached 1113 km before).
