## Measured (from `tmp/explore.ts`, the same pipeline, decoded via `buildWorldField`)

- Rings reach 0.341 (x), 0.267 (y) of the side from the middle: inside 0.40.
- Bounds dome: centred, both radii clamp to 0.26 (half-extents 0.341, 0.267).
- **A clipped distance cannot be fitted.** The `|k| ≤ 1.5` modes allow
  `|c| ≤ 6.338 · 0.01959 = 0.124`. Clipping makes the far sea flat while the
  dome keeps falling, so `k = 0` and `|k| = 1` blow past that budget. Every clip
  of 20–1000 km gives 50–95 % wrong area, with 3 words clipped. The unclipped
  distance, at one field unit per `r·side` (2,704 km), matches the dome's far
  slope: 11.75 % wrong, with only `k = (1, 0)` clipped (factor 6.67). At unit
  2,575 km (×1.05) the factor is 6.37 and wrong area is **1.22 %** (+937 −240
  nodes of 96,772). The leftover `kx = 1` comes from the island's mass sitting
  west of the square's centre, so moving the dome's centreX toward −0.04 should
  clear it. That move is what the fitted dome is for.
- Clip 2,500 km at ×1.2 gives 14.8 %, and the wrong share swings hard with
  scale. The curve is in `fit-world.ts`'s output once it runs.
