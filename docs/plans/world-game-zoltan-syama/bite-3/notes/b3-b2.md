## Decisions and numbers

- **Pole rule in sampling departs from `b3-map.md` § 2.2**: bilinear in (θ, λ)
  across ring −1 gives `(g(λ) + g(λ+π))/2` at the pole, which depends on λ. The
  sampler instead runs linear in θ from the outermost ring to `poles[i]` (the
  ring's mean), continuous at the pole. The contour and the sweep use the ring
  −1 = ring 0 at λ+π rule as designed.
- **Two sweep passes** (`PASSES` in `sphere-sweep.ts`): one pass errs up to 1.6
  cells (126 km at 256×512) near a cap's antipode; two bring every cap within
  0.06 cell (4.4 km); a third changes nothing. Ring sweeps continue past λ = 0
  until a node keeps its own segment.
- **Timing, Node, this container, world 2, L = 511 on 1024 × 2048**: roll ~120
  ms, synthesis ~350 ms, contour 100–250 ms, distance 400–650 ms, total
  **1.0–1.4 s** (world 3: 1.25 s). Within the 3 s budget.
- Tests: `coast-field/model/sphere-field.test.ts` (wrap/pole indexing, node
  positions vs `latLonToUnit`, sampling continuous across λ = 0, at both poles
  and into the caps); `world/model/sphere-world.test.ts` (20° caps —
  mid-latitude, both poles, date line, λ = 0 — within a cell of R·(20° − angle);
  contour on the cap edge; the timing diagnostic; `source` honoured).
