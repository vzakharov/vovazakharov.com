# b3-c3 — Earth's envelope, measured and framed

Branch `wt/b3-c3`, base `b77a99442`. Step C3 of `b3-c-map.md` § 7.

## What landed

- `pnpm spectrum:island [--source row|target]` (`scripts/earth-spectrum.ts`):
  `A(l) = √(Σ_m c_lm² / (2l+1))` of #0's series, fitted
  `(knee² + l²)^(−slope/2)` in `ln A` both ways (call 7), plus the probe's held
  at knee 6, slope 2 with only its amplitude fitted. Row and unquantized target
  give the same fits to the printed digits.
- `scripts/lib/envelope-fit.ts` + test: `degreeAmplitudes`, `equalWeights`,
  `logWeights` (degree `l` weighs `ln((l+½)/(l−½))`, bins equal in `log l` in
  the narrow-bin limit), `fitEnvelope` (knee grid 0.1–40 by 0.05, weighted least
  squares per knee), `holdEnvelope`.
- `EARTH_ENVELOPE = kneeEnvelope(5.3, 2.136)` in `land-series.ts`, exported
  through the land, world and terrain barrels; **not the default** (C4).
  `harmonicSlot` exported from the world and terrain barrels for the script.
- `frame:island --views [--size 480]` (call 9a): each world as two orthographic
  globes over the equator, at 0° and 180° E (`scripts/lib/globe-view.ts` + test,
  east to the right). Earth draws right way round (Africa/Europe; Pacific with
  Australia lower left).

## The fit

| Weighting           | Knee | Slope | Residual (ln A) |
| ------------------- | ---: | ----: | --------------: |
| bins equal in log l | 5.30 | 2.136 |           0.234 |
| every degree equal  | 8.90 | 2.327 |           0.097 |
| probe, held         |    6 |     2 |           0.369 |

The equal-weight fit matches the map's probe (knee 8, slope 2.29 at clip 500);
log bins pull the knee down to 5.3 and the slope to 2.14 because l = 1–4 carry
more amplitude than a knee of 8.9 allows (fit/measured at l = 1: 0.81 log bins,
0.47 equal, 0.56 probe). At the tail the probe's slope 2 overshoots Earth: 2.07×
the measured A at l = 511, 1.79× at 300; both fits stay within 0.95–1.27 there.

## Frames (`b3-c3/`)

Worlds #2–#6, top to bottom, at orbit over 0° (left) and 180° (right):
`globes-probe.png` (6, 2), `globes-earth.png` (5.3, 2.136, `EARTH_ENVELOPE`),
`globes-earth-equal.png` (8.9, 2.327, for the record).

**Earth's envelope gives planets, not lace.** Same seeds, same layout as the
probe's: solid continents with a few inland seas, coasts a shade smoother, small
islands fewer, and neighbouring masses fusing — #5's and #9's into one
supercontinent (83%, 94% of the land). The equal-weight envelope cuts the other
way: more channels, more mid-sized continents, largest 20–50%.

## For the lead (call 8)

Earth's envelope (log bins: knee 5.3, slope 2.136) gives planets, closer to
Earth's own count and largest share than the probe's; its cost is more
supercontinent worlds (#5, #9, #14, #21 over 80%). The every-degree fit (8.9,
2.327) would give more, smaller continents than Earth has. C4 switches
`sphere-world.ts` and `frame-worlds.ts` defaults to whichever you pick.
