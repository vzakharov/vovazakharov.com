## Left: `fillCubeTile` (`tile/model/cube-fill.ts`)

The design, ready to build:

- **Lattice**: build `(grid+2)²` points with `N = 2^level·cells`. A point's
  global indices are `kU = x·cells + i`, `kV = y·cells + j`, for `i, j ∈ [−1,
  cells+1]`. When both are in `[0, N]`, the point is
  `faceToSphere(face, faceLattice(level, cells, kU), faceLattice(…, kV))`.
- **One index off the face** (`−1` or `N+1`): look up `EDGES[face][dir]` to
  get `{face: beyond, back, flip}`. The along index is `flip ? N − k : k`. The
  perpendicular index sits one row inside the beyond face, at `N−1` if `back`
  is `right`/`up` and at `1` otherwise. This is the same expression the
  neighbour tile uses, so the points are bit-equal.
- **Both off** happens only at the 4 outer-ring corners at a cube corner, and
  only the skirt reads them. There, the two ring neighbours `at(−1,0)` and
  `at(0,−1)` land on the same point, one step along the third edge. Use that
  point; it gives a diagonal outward flare of one cell. The corner vertex's
  central-difference normal then comes from the three real edge neighbours
  without any special case.
- **Elevation and sizes**: world is `p·(R + h)`, and the middle is
  `faceToSphere(mid)·R`. Spacing is `cubeTileSide(key)/cells`. The sample is
  `(p, finest) => h`, and the option `finest?: (p: Vec3) => number` matches
  `cubeDetailCut`.
- **DRY**: move the normals, skirt, buffers and `triangulate` out of
  `tile-fill.ts` into a shared mesh builder that takes `at(i, j)` and `middle`,
  with `grid` and `skirtDepth` in a base options type (type-overlap). Keep the
  planar output bit-identical and check it with `tmp/b4/golden.ts`, which
  holds the mesh golden.
- **Tests**: tile pairs across all 12 face edges plus in-face pairs. The
  float64 lattice and the heights should be bit-equal, and the normals equal
  to 1e-6. Check the corner vertex's normal from all three tiles at a cube
  corner, then time the fill against the planar fill.
